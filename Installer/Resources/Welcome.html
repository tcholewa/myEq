Install myEq

myEq equalizes your Mac’s mixed stereo output. This installer adds the app, its virtual output driver, and audio helpers.

Quit myEq and audio playback apps, including Music, before continuing.

If the driver or bridge changed, Core Audio will reload at the end of installation. Sound in all apps will briefly stop, including calls and recordings. Install when this interruption is acceptable. Updates with unchanged driver and bridge identities keep Core Audio running, but replacing the driver files may still unload the virtual output and require a manual audio-service reload. If audio apps remain stuck after a reload, save your work and restart your Mac.

After installation, open myEq from Applications and allow its background helper if macOS asks. Reopen your playback apps so they reconnect to Core Audio. If Music skips tracks after an update, quit Music completely with Command-Q, then reopen it. Your presets are preserved.

Install myEq

myEq equalizes your Mac’s mixed stereo output. This installer adds the app, its virtual output driver, and audio helpers.

Quit myEq before continuing.

At the end of installation, Core Audio will reload. Sound in all apps will briefly stop, including calls and recordings. Install when this interruption is acceptable. You do not need to restart your Mac.

After installation, open myEq from Applications and allow its background helper if macOS asks. Your presets are preserved.
